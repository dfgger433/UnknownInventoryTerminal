Unmodified PNGs (AE2 -> local):
- guis/text_field.png -> widgets/text_field.png
- guis/cpu_selector.png -> settings/cpu_selector.png
- guis/checkbox.png -> settings/toggle.png
- gui/sprites/button.png -> widgets/button.png
- gui/sprites/button_highlighted.png -> widgets/button_highlighted.png
- gui/sprites/big_scroller.png -> storage/scroll_thumb.png
- gui/sprites/big_scroller_disabled.png -> storage/scroll_thumb_disabled.png
- gui/sprites/vertical_buttons_bg.png -> widgets/vertical_button_bar.png

Adapted PNGs (AE2 -> local):
- guis/terminal.png -> terminal/terminal_background.png (reworked layout)
- guis/states.png -> terminal/terminal_controls.png (edited button/icon atlas)
- guis/background.png -> settings/settings_background.png (resized and edited)
- gui/sprites/vertical_buttons_bg.png -> widgets/vertical_button_bar_bottom.png
  (edited seam) and widgets/vertical_button_bar_variant.png (reconstructed from
  a 21x26 pixel table; the previous variant's PNG bytes were not recovered)
- gui/sprites/button.png, gui/sprites/button_highlighted.png, and
  gui/sprites/button_disabled.png -> widgets/drop_down_list.png (operator-
  supplied atlas; matching button faces cropped to 112 pixels and arranged
  into field/option states, with chevrons and row edits)
